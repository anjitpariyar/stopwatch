# Privacy Policy: Unlocked

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Unlocked counts how many times you unlock your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## What the app reads
With your permission ("Usage access" in Android settings), Unlocked reads the device unlock events that Android already records. It uses only the time of each unlock, to count unlocks per day. It does not read which apps you use, your messages, your location or any other personal data.

## Where your data lives
Your unlock counts are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded, sold or shared. Unlocked has no account system, no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes all recorded history. You can withdraw Usage access at any time in Settings → Usage access.

## Children
Unlocked is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
